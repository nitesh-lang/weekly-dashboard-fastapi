❔ How the margin is calculated

The one-line idea: take the selling price, subtract everything it costs us to sell one unit — the product, shipping it to India, Amazon's fees, ads, GST, overheads. Whatever is left is our profit (net margin).

Where every number comes from

NumberSourceFilled how
FOB, freight, duty %, additional cost, NLCBrand master sheetManual (sourcing team)
USD rate, surcharge %, GST slab, overhead %, finance %Edit ParametersManual (set rarely)

Referral / fulfilment / closing feeAmazon fee API, at that priceAuto — refreshable

Auto fields fill once when you pick a SKU so the screen starts from reality — after that every box is editable for what-ifs. Nothing you type is saved to the master.

Step 1 — Landed cost (DP)

The true cost of getting one piece from the factory into our warehouse: factory price + freight (both $ × USD rate), import duty on that total, a surcharge on the duty, plus any per-unit extras.

DP = FOB₹ + Freight₹ + Duty + Surcharge + Additional cost

Step 2 — GST hiding inside the price

Part of the customer's price is tax that was never our money. On ₹999 at 18% slab, ₹152 goes to the government.

GST = SP − (SP ÷ 1.18) ← per-SKU slab if set, else default

Step 3 — Gross margin (the simple check)

Gross = (SP − GST) − DP

If gross is thin, no ads discipline will save the SKU — the price or the cost has to move.

Step 4 — Everything else → Net margin

CostCharged asPlain words
Referral fee% of SPAmazon's commission
Fulfilment + closing fee₹ fixedAmazon delivering the order
AMS (ads)% of SPAd spend per rupee of sales = TACOS
Coupon% of SPDiscount we fund
Returns loss% of SPMoney lost on returns/damage
SMM% of SPSocial-media marketing, if any
Overhead + finance% of NLCOffice/salaries + cost of money locked in stock
Net margin = SP − (DP + all costs above + GST)

Net margin % (of SP) is the number that decides if the SKU makes money. The waterfall panel on the right shows exactly this flow, cost by cost.

Worked example (₹999 SKU)

Selling price999.00
− Landed cost DP (FOB $4 + freight $0.50 @ ₹84, 20% duty, 10% surcharge, ₹10 extra)−471.16
− GST inside price (18%)−152.39
Gross margin375.45 (37.6%)
− Referral 16% / fulfilment ₹40 / closing ₹25−224.84
− Ads 8% / returns 1%−89.91
− Overhead 2% + finance 1.5% of NLC ₹500−17.50
Net margin₹43.20 (4.3%)
Same SKU with ads at 12% instead of 8% → net drops to about ₹3. That is why the tool pulls your actual TACOS instead of a guess.
